Experimental Design

Sample groups, organ selection, treatment conditions, controls, and study timeline.

Overview

We designed the experiment with two time cohorts: day 10 and day 40. Each cohort contains two groups: the rapamycin treated group and the control group. After the designated treatment period, we use CO2 to knock down the flies, dissect them under a microscope, perform fixation and washing, and image them with spontaneous Raman spectroscopy.

Data Collection on Organs

Gut

For gut from Drosophila, we acquired spontaneous Raman spectra of the lipid droplets from the R2 region of the gut because it is most relevant to the digestive system.

Ovary

For ovary from Drosophila, we acquired spontaneous Raman spectra of the lipid droplets from stage 8 eggs, since this is during the stage of development and proliferation.

Fatbody

For fatbody from Drosophila, we acquired spontaneous Raman spectra of the lipid droplets from fatbody cells, which are uniform cells.
