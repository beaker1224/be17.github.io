Pipeline Overview

The data pipeline converts raw spontaneous Raman spectra into interpretable organ-specific metabolic readouts. After collecting spectra from lipid droplets, the workflow organizes samples by organ, age cohort, and treatment group, then applies preprocessing steps such as quality control, baseline correction, normalization, and spectral comparison. The processed data are then used to generate spectra plots, ratio-matrix summaries, and biological interpretations of how rapamycin may reshape metabolism during the Drosophila aging process.
